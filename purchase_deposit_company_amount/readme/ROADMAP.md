The deposit is relieved in full on the first bill that follows it, so the
difference between the deposit as paid and the exchange rate can only be
charged to the goods without distorting them when those goods are the whole
order. Billing an order in instalments is therefore not supported: the bill
that carries the deposit offset must invoice every line of the order.

Posting a bill that leaves part of the order unbilled is refused with an
explanation. Clearing the Company Currency Amount on the deposit bill opts
out and restores the standard exchange rate conversion throughout.

Supporting instalments would mean spreading the difference over the whole
ordered quantity and carrying the unabsorbed part forward to later bills,
which have no deposit offset line left to hold it.
