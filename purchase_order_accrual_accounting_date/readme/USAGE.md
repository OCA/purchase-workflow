Before creating an accrual entry, you need:

- A user with the *Accountant* rights (*Invoicing > Show Full Accounting
  Features*), required to see the *Accrued Expense Entry* action.
- A journal of type *Miscellaneous*, where the accrual entry and its reversal
  are posted.
- An account of type *Current Liabilities* to use as the accrual account.
- Confirmed purchase orders whose products have an expense account, either on
  the product, on its category or as the company default.

To create the accrual entry:

1. Go to *Purchase > Orders > Purchase Orders* and select the orders to accrue.
2. Click *Actions > Accrued Expense Entry*.
3. Set the accrual date, the reversal date, the journal and the accrual account.
4. Check the preview and click *Create Entry*.

With *Accrued Expenses by Accounting Date* enabled, only bills with an
accounting date on or before the accrual date reduce the amount to accrue.
