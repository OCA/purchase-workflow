To configure this module, you need to:

1.  Configure your service product with the flag *Subcontracted Service*
    in the product form if this product should trigger a procurement.
    The product must be purchasable, ensure *Purchase* is checked.
2.  Add the supplier information in your product form in the *Purchase*
    tab.
3.  Additionally and despite a predefined rule is created in each
    warehouse, you can configure the "Subcontracting Service Procurement
    Rule" for each warehouse through:

    - Inventory
    - Configuration
    - Warehouse Management
    - Warehouse

    You must be a member of the technical group *Manage Push and Pull
    inventory flows* (`stock.group_adv_location`)
