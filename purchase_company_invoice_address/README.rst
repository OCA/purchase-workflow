This module lets you select, on each purchase order, the address of your **own**
company to which the vendor should send the invoice, and prints it on the
purchase order report.

Odoo links a company to a single partner record, so a company operating from
several locations has no standard place to hold more than one of its own
addresses. This module reuses the child addresses of the company partner for
that purpose: the head office (the company partner itself) and any of its
address records can be selected per purchase order.

The selected address is carried over to the vendor bills created from the
purchase order.

Configuration
=============

No configuration is needed to start using the module: as long as your company
has no dedicated invoice address, purchase orders propose the address of the
company itself.

To offer additional locations:

#. Go to *Contacts* and open the contact of your company.
#. In the *Contacts & Addresses* tab, create an address for the location and
   set its *Address Type* to *Invoice Address*.

Only the company itself and its invoice addresses can be selected on a purchase
order. Once an invoice address exists it becomes the address proposed by default
on new orders, while the company address stays selectable.

Usage
=====

#. Go to *Purchase > Orders > Purchase Orders* and create an order.
#. Set *Company Invoice Address* to the address of your company the vendor
   should send the invoice to.
#. Print the order: the address is shown along with the other order
   information.
#. Create a vendor bill from the order: it carries the same address.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

* The address is printed on the purchase order report only, neither on the
  request for quotation report nor on the vendor bill report.
* Purchase orders of the same vendor, company and currency are merged into a
  single vendor bill by Odoo. When such orders carry different invoice
  addresses, the address of the first order is used for the bill.
