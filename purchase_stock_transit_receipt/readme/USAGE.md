On a purchase order, the operation type follows the partner and the
incoterm: the warehouse's Transit receipt type when the incoterm is
flagged *Transit Receipt*, its usual receipt type otherwise. It can
still be changed by hand, until the partner or the incoterm changes.

Validate the Transit receipt when the vendor ships the goods; the usual
reception is then ready in the warehouse.

A purchase generated by a procurement (make to order, reordering rule)
gets the Transit receipt type on the same terms, and a new procurement
is added to a draft purchase of the same vendor using either type. In
make to order, the customer delivery waits for the usual reception: it
never reserves goods in the Incoming transit. It is still linked to the
Transit receipt: a late Transit receipt raises its delay alert, deadline
changes are propagated both ways, and cancelling the Transit receipt
sets it back to make to stock.

Other modules can extend `purchase.order._requires_transit_receipt()`
to send more purchases through the Incoming transit.

The received quantity of the purchase changes only when the goods leave
the Incoming transit into the warehouse: it stays at 0 while the goods
are in transit, and returning the usual reception to the Incoming
transit decreases it.
