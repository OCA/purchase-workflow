Every warehouse gets an **Incoming transit** location and a **Transit
receipt** operation type. A purchase using this operation type is
received in two or more steps:

1. the Transit receipt, from the vendor to the Incoming transit, when
   the vendor ships the goods;
2. the usual reception of the warehouse (one, two or three steps),
   created by a push rule when the Transit receipt is done.

The Incoming transit is a transit location of the warehouse's company,
outside the warehouse: goods in transit are valued and counted in the
inventory, but they are neither available nor reservable.

This is the design inspired by ``stock_route_transit`` (OCA
stock-logistics-transport, 8.0 to 10.0), without new reception steps.

Configuration
=============

Tick *Transit Receipt* on the incoterms whose purchases must go through
the Incoming transit (Purchase > Configuration > Incoterms), for
instance those where the buyer bears the transport risk.

Usage
=====

On a purchase order, the operation type follows the partner and the
incoterm: the warehouse's Transit receipt type when the incoterm is
flagged *Transit Receipt*, its usual receipt type otherwise. It can
still be changed by hand, until the partner or the incoterm changes.

Validate the Transit receipt when the vendor ships the goods; the usual
reception is then ready in the warehouse.

Other modules can extend ``purchase.order._requires_transit_receipt()``
to send more purchases through the Incoming transit.
