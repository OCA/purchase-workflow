To use this module, you need to:

1. Go to a vendor form view, and set its terms and conditions in the "Sales & Purchase" notebook tab, at the botton of the "Purchase" group 
2. Create a purchase order for that vendor. In the terms and conditions of the form view you will see the vendor terms and conditions, you can edit the field for the specific purchase.
3. Print the purchase report, in the terms and conditions you will see the vendor conditions.
