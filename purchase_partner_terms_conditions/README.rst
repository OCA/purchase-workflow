This module adds a custom terms and conditions fields in the vendors
that will be propagated to purchases

Configuration
=============

To configure this module, you need to:

-  Go to Purchase / Configuration / Settings, search the "Partner Terms
   and Conditions Commercial Field" check, and unmark it if you do not
   want contact company's conditions to be propagated to their
   individuals and addresses

Usage
=====

To use this module, you need to:

1. Go to a vendor form view, and set its terms and conditions in the
   "Sales & Purchase" notebook tab, at the botton of the "Purchase"
   group
2. Create a purchase order for that vendor. In the terms and conditions
   of the form view you will see the vendor terms and conditions, you
   can edit the field for the specific purchase.
3. Print the purchase report, in the terms and conditions you will see
   the vendor conditions.
