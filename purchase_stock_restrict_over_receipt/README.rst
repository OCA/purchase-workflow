This module blocks the validation of a receipt that would leave a
purchase order line received beyond its ordered quantity.

Odoo accepts any quantity on a receipt: the excess is added to the
received quantity of the order line, and a product that was not ordered
gets a new order line with no ordered quantity. Whatever is derived from
the received quantity, such as a vendor bill on received quantities,
then follows the receipt rather than the order.

With this module the order has to be updated first, so that an excess is
agreed on the order before it enters the stock.

Usage
=====

Validate a receipt of a purchase order as usual. The validation is
refused, and nothing is received, when:

- the received quantity of an order line would exceed its ordered
  quantity, counting the earlier receipts and returns of the line, or
- the receipt has a product that is not on the purchase order.

To receive the excess, update the purchase order first: raise the
ordered quantity of the line, or add a line for the product. The demand
of the open receipt follows the order. A line that was added by hand on
the receipt is kept next to it, and has to be removed before validating.

Returns to the vendor and receipts without a purchase order are not
restricted.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- Kits are checked on the received quantity of the kit: an excess on
  some of the components only is not detected.
- The check runs when the receipt is processed, so a receipt that also
  has a line short of its demand shows the backorder dialog before it is
  refused.
- A quantity changed on a receipt after its validation is not checked.
