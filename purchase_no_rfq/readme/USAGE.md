To use this module, you need to:

- Go to Purchase > Orders > Purchase Orders. All the orders are 
listed here, including the ones in 'Draft' and 'Sent' state.
- Click on New and select a vendor. The title of the form is 
'Purchase Order' and not 'Request for Quotation'.
- Add the products you want to purchase.
- Click on Send by Email to send the order to the vendor. 
The order moves to the 'Sent' state.
- Click on Confirm Order. The order moves to the 'Purchase Order' 
state. You can also confirm it directly from the 'Draft' state.
- Use the Print menu to print the purchase order. The title of 
the document is 'Purchase Order', and the 'Request for Quotation' 
option is not available.

The menu 'Purchase > Orders > Requests for Quotation' is 
only visible for users with the technical features enabled 
(developer mode).
