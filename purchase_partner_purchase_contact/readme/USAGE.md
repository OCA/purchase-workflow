Mark a contact as a purchase contact by selecting the **Purchase** address
type when creating a child contact of a vendor:

![Purchase address type on a contact](static/description/contact_type_purchase.png)

On a request for quotation or purchase order, pick the contact in the
**Purchase Contact** field. Only the vendor's purchase contacts are offered:

![Purchase Contact field on a purchase order](static/description/purchase_order_purchase_contact.png)

When sending the RFQ or purchase order by email, the purchase contact is
used as the default recipient (falling back to the vendor company when no
contact is set or it has no email address):

![Purchase contact as default email recipient](static/description/email_default_recipient.png)
