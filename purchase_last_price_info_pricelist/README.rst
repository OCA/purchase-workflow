This module adds a new "Based on" option to pricelist rules: **Last
Purchase Price**. The base price of the rule is the unit price, net of
discount, of the most recent confirmed purchase order line of the
product. It is converted to the unit of measure and the currency being
priced.

Only purchases of the pricelist's company are considered, or of the
current company when the pricelist is shared between companies, so a
purchase made by another company never sets the price.

Products that were never purchased fall back to their cost.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Sales > Products > Pricelists* and open or create a pricelist.
2. Add a rule with *Price Type* = *Formula* and *Based on* = *Last
   Purchase Price*.
3. Set the markup, for example 50% to sell at 1.5 times the last
   purchase price.

When a product is priced with this pricelist, the base price is the last
confirmed purchase price of the product.
