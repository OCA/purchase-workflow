1. Go to *Sales > Products > Pricelists* and open or create a pricelist.
2. Add a rule with *Price Type* = *Formula* and *Based on* = *Last Purchase
   Price*.
3. Set the markup, for example 50% to sell at 1.5 times the last purchase
   price.

When a product is priced with this pricelist, the base price is the last
confirmed purchase price of the product.
